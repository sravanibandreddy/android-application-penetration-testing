Privacy Policy

Android Security Scanner

Last Updated: November 5, 2026

1. Introduction

Android Security Scanner is designed to analyze installed applications, user-selected APK files, and PDF documents for potential security risks.

2. Information Accessed

To provide its security analysis features, the application may access:

  • Installed application names and package identifiers.
  • Application-requested permissions and their grant status.
  • APK files and related metadata selected by the user.
  • PDF documents and document metadata selected by the user.

3. Data Processing and Storage

Security analysis is designed to take place locally on the device. The application does not intentionally upload scanned files or application information to our servers.

Selected APK and PDF files are temporarily copied into the application's private cache for analysis. These temporary copies are deleted after the scanning operation completes.

The application does not intentionally maintain a permanent database of scan results or installed application information.

4. Reports and File Export

Users can generate PDF security reports and choose their preferred destination using the Android document picker. Reports are written to the destination selected by the user.

Any external file provider or cloud storage service selected by the user may process files according to that provider's own privacy practices.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information. The application does not intentionally transmit scanned documents or installed application information to our servers or share them with third parties.

Users remain responsible for destinations and external services they choose when importing or exporting files.

6. Security

The application uses Android's private application cache for temporary scan copies and deletes those copies after scanning. Reasonable measures are used to protect information processed by the application. However, absolute security cannot be guaranteed.

7. Children's Privacy

The application is not specifically directed at children and is not designed to knowingly collect children's personal information.

8. Policy Changes

This Privacy Policy may be updated when application features or privacy practices change. The updated policy will be published on this page.
